Ask on WhatsApp
How long does a project take? +
Brand identity: 7–14 working days. Social media creatives: 3–5 days. Packaging: 10–15 days. Presentations: 5–7 days. Rush timelines available — always confirmed before we start.
Do you work with clients outside Delhi? +
Absolutely. We're a remote-first studio. Clients across India and internationally — all communication over WhatsApp, email, and video calls. File delivery is always digital.
How many revisions are included? +
Unlimited revisions on the chosen concept direction. We don't nickel-and-dime revisions. Our goal is that you're genuinely happy — not just tolerating it.
What do you need to get started? +
Your business name, what you do, who your customers are, brands you admire, and your deadline. We guide you through the rest in a discovery call. No detailed brief needed.
Do I own the final designs? +
Yes, 100%. Once the project is complete and paid for, all rights transfer to you. Source files and full commercial usage rights — no licensing fees, no attribution required.
What software do you use? +
Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop, and InDesign for primary design work. Figma for UI and presentations. Canva templates for client-editable social media systems. AI tools (Midjourney, Grok) for AI-directed art — always art-directed by a human designer.
What is your payment process? +
50% upfront and 50% on final delivery. Larger projects can have milestone-based payments. We accept UPI, bank transfer, and Razorpay. GST invoices provided.
